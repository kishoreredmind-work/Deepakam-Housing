Project Overview

Villa DEEPVKVM — Muttikulangara - Palakkad brings luxury independent villa living to Muttikulangara, Palakkad. These premium 3 BHK & 4 BHK villas feature clay tile sloping roofs in authentic Kerala architectural styles, private compound gardens, security gates, and pure drinking water connections. The 4 BHK villas are completely sold out, and only 3 BHK units are now available. RERA Registered (Registration No: K-RERA/PRJ/PKD/127/2023).
